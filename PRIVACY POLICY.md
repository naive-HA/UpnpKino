# Privacy Policy for UPnP Kino

**Last updated: August 12, 2026**

## Summary

UPnP Kino does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. Everything it touches stays on your device and within your private local network.

## What UPnP Kino Does

- **Acts as a UPnP/DLNA Media Server.** It hosts a built-in HTTP server to make your selected local media (movies and music) available to other devices on your private local network.
- **Acts as a DLNA and Chromecast Controller.** It discovers compatible media players on your local network and allows you to start, stop, and control playback of your media on those devices.

## What UPnP Kino Doesn't Do

- No account creation or sign-in required.
- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
- No ads or ad-tracking SDKs.
- No cloud storage or remote access—your media never leaves your local network.
- No personal data is shared with third parties.

## Permissions

- **Local Network & Wi-Fi State:** Used exclusively to discover and communicate with DLNA and Chromecast devices on the same Wi-Fi network.
- **Storage (Photos, Video, Audio):** Used to read and stream the media files you choose to include in your library.
- **Notifications:** Used to display a persistent notification while the media server is active, allowing you to quickly stop the service.
- **Foreground Service:** Required to keep the media server running so playback isn't interrupted when you switch to other apps.

## Data Retention

UPnP Kino doesn't run any external servers and has nothing to retain. All configuration data (such as your selected folder paths) lives locally on your device; uninstalling the app removes all associated data.

## Third-Party Services

If you install UPnP Kino from Google Play, Google collects some data as part of operating the Play Store (e.g. install counts and OS-level crash data) under Google's own privacy policy—this is outside UPnP Kino's control.

UPnP Kino itself uses no third-party SDKs or services.

## Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because UPnP Kino doesn't collect personal data, there's no data of yours for us to give you access to, correct, delete, or export.

## Children's Privacy

UPnP Kino is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect personal data.

## Changes to This Policy

If UPnP Kino's data practices change in the future, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
